TaharaCo — Project Aswan Brief

Project Aswan is a client consulting project at TaharaCo focused on improving logistics operations for a regional retail company.

The core teams involved are the Strategy Team and the Data & Operations Team.

Yara Hassan is the Project Lead for Project Aswan. She works with the Strategy Team and reports to Omar Nabil, Director of Consulting.

The Data & Operations Team supports the project by analyzing delivery performance and operational data. Karim Adel is the team's data analyst and reports to Salma Farouk, Head of Data & Operations.

The Strategy Team is responsible for the client roadmap, business recommendations, and final project specification. The Data & Operations Team is responsible for validating metrics, operational assumptions, and data-related sections of the specification.

The final Project Aswan specification should be reviewed by Yara Hassan for project scope and by Salma Farouk for operational and data accuracy. Omar Nabil provides final management oversight.

TaharaCo uses Delta Analytics as a vendor for logistics data services. Delta Analytics provides delivery-performance datasets used by the Data & Operations Team.

The final specification should not be approved until both the strategy scope and operational data sections have been reviewed.
