TaharaCo — Vendor and Final Specification Notes

Delta Analytics is an external vendor that provides logistics and delivery-performance datasets to TaharaCo.

Delta Analytics supports Project Aswan through the Data & Operations Team. Karim Adel uses Delta Analytics datasets to validate delivery metrics, while Nour Ibrahim uses the same data to check operational assumptions.

The Project Aswan final specification contains three major sections: business strategy, operational design, and data validation.

Yara Hassan owns the business strategy section and is responsible for confirming that the proposed recommendations match the client's goals.

Salma Farouk owns the operational design and data validation sections. She must verify delivery metrics, operational assumptions, and data quality before the final specification is approved.

Karim Adel should review the detailed delivery metrics with Salma Farouk before they are included in the final specification.

Nour Ibrahim should review operational assumptions with Salma Farouk.

The recommended final review process is:
1. Karim Adel validates the delivery metrics with Delta Analytics data.
2. Nour Ibrahim validates the operational assumptions.
3. Salma Farouk reviews the complete operational and data sections.
4. Yara Hassan reviews the business strategy and overall project scope.
5. Omar Nabil provides final management oversight.

A final specification review should involve both Yara Hassan and Salma Farouk because they cover different parts of Project Aswan. Omar Nabil should be consulted for final approval.
